A Little Note

From my heart to yours

My dearest Darksha,

On the 29th of March, two names were written together in a single line — Naseeb and Darksha. What began as a Nikah, a promise spoken before Allah, has quietly become my whole world.

You are the calm in my mornings and the du'a in my nights. With you, an ordinary day becomes a blessing, and a hard day becomes bearable. Allah gave me in you what no wealth could ever buy — sakinah.

If a half century of love is what lies ahead, then let it come. I will spend every one of those years choosing you again — in ease and in hardship, in laughter and in silence, in this world and in the hope of the next.

Thank you for saying yes. Thank you for staying. Thank you for being mine.

Yours, always —
Naseeb Your husband, your friend, your home
